CAPPUCCINO-COFFEE

It is a coffee drink made from:

~Espresso

~Steamed milk

~Milk foam

- 35 L.E for the small box

- 60 L.E for the big box

CLASSIC-COFFEE

It's plain coffee with almost no additions (or very simple additions like sugar or a little milk), and it focuses on the taste of the coffee itself.

- 40 L.E for the small box

- 80 L.E for the big box

CLASSIC-ICED-COFFEE

It's the same idea as plain coffee, but:

1-It's first made hot.

2-then it cools down.

3-served on ice.

- 60 L.E for the small box

- 100 L.E for the big box

COFFEE-WITH-MILK

It's regular coffee (plain or espresso) with added milk, which makes the taste:

~Milder

~Less bitter

~Creamy and smooth

- 70 L.E for the small box

- 120 L.E for the big box

COLD-BREW

1-Its taste is smooth and less bitter than regular coffee.

2-Very refreshing, especially in the heat.

3-It has a slightly high caffeine content and provides energy.

- 45 L.E for the small box

- 100 L.E for the big box

ICED-COFFEE-MOCHA

1-Suitable for those who don't like the bitterness of coffee.

2-Very refreshing in the summer.

3-Has the energy of caffeine + a sweet chocolate flavor.

- 60 L.E for the small box

- 120 L.E for the big box

ICED-LATTEE

1-The coffee tastes light and smooth.

2-The milk makes it creamy and not bitter.

3-It's less sweet than mocha (unless you add sugar).

- 80 L.E for the small box

- 130 L.E for the big box

MILKED-COFFEE

1-Its taste is smooth and light.

2-Less bitter than black coffee.

3-Similar to the taste of a latte, but simpler and less "pretty" to prepare.

- 50 L.E for the small box

- 100 L.E for the big box

MOCHA-COFFEE

1-A sweet and creamy taste.

2-The chocolate masks the bitterness of the coffee.

3-More like a "dessert" drink with caffeine.

- 60 L.E for the small box

- 110 L.E for the big box

VANILLA-LATTEE

1-Very smooth and creamy taste.

2-It has a subtle vanilla sweetness.

3-The coffee isn't too strong (milder than an Americano, for example).

- 65 L.E for the small box

- 120 L.E for the big box
